# UnitedMC — Code of Conduct

**Last Updated:** October 5, 2026

The UnitedMC community is built around having fun, playing Minecraft, and giving people a place to hang out. We expect everyone to help keep the community welcoming and enjoyable.

This Code of Conduct applies to the UnitedMC community, including the Minecraft server, Discord server, GitHub community spaces, and other official UnitedMC spaces where applicable.

## Our Expectations

Community members should:

- Treat others with respect.
- Communicate without harassment, bullying, or threats.
- Avoid racism, sexism, hate speech, and discriminatory behaviour.
- Keep content, usernames, and avatars appropriate.
- Avoid unnecessary spam, flooding, and disruption.
- Respect other people's privacy.
- Use the appropriate channels for discussions.
- Follow reasonable instructions from UnitedMC staff.
- Help keep the community welcoming for new members.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment or targeted bullying.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Hate speech or discriminatory behaviour.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting community spaces.
- Repeatedly attempting to bypass moderation actions.
- Other behaviour that seriously harms the UnitedMC community.

## Moderation

UnitedMC staff may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct or the UnitedMC rules are violated.

Actions may include warnings, removal of content, mutes, kicks, temporary restrictions, or bans.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it through the official UnitedMC Discord server:

**Discord:** https://dsc.gg/unitedmc

Please provide enough information for the staff team to understand what happened.

## Good Faith

Reports should be made honestly and in good faith. Deliberately making false reports or abusing the reporting system may itself result in moderation action.

## Changes

This Code of Conduct may be updated as the UnitedMC community develops.
