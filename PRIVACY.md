# UnitedMC — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use UnitedMC and how that information may be used.

UnitedMC uses Minecraft plugins, authentication systems, Discord bots, and other services to operate and manage the community. Information may therefore be processed or stored by these systems rather than by one central UnitedMC database.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on how you interact with UnitedMC, the following information may be processed.

### Minecraft Information

This may include:

- Minecraft username
- Minecraft UUID
- IP address
- Authentication information
- Player statistics and gameplay data
- Punishment or moderation information
- Other information required by installed Minecraft plugins

### Discord Information

If you interact with UnitedMC through Discord or connect your Discord account to UnitedMC services, our Discord bots or integrations may process information such as:

- Discord user ID
- Discord username
- Display name
- Avatar information
- Server membership information
- Messages or command-related information where necessary for a feature or moderation system

The exact information processed depends on the features and integrations currently in use.

## 2. Passwords

UnitedMC does not intentionally store player passwords in plain text.

When authentication is required, passwords are handled by the authentication system used by the server and are stored using a one-way password hashing mechanism rather than as the original password.

You should never use a password on UnitedMC that you also use for important personal accounts.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide and operate UnitedMC services.
- Authenticate players.
- Maintain player accounts and gameplay data.
- Moderate the Minecraft server and Discord community.
- Apply and enforce punishments.
- Prevent cheating, abuse, spam, and other harmful activity.
- Investigate security or moderation incidents.
- Maintain and improve server features.
- Protect the security and stability of UnitedMC.

## 4. IP Addresses

IP addresses may be processed by the Minecraft server, hosting infrastructure, authentication systems, or plugins.

They may be used when reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

- Authentication and account security.
- Preventing abuse.
- Enforcing IP-based punishments.
- Investigating security incidents.
- Maintaining server security.

## 5. Third-Party Services

UnitedMC may use third-party hosting providers, plugins, Discord bots, APIs, and other services.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their functionality.

UnitedMC does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is generally retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, moderate, and maintain UnitedMC and its services.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is used.

## 7. Data Security

UnitedMC takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by its systems.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability affecting UnitedMC, please report it privately through the official UnitedMC Discord server rather than publicly disclosing the issue.

## 8. Personal Information

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information, including passwords, addresses, phone numbers, or other private information, in public UnitedMC channels.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as UnitedMC develops. The latest version will be available through the official UnitedMC public repository.
